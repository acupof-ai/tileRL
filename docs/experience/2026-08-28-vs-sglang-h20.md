# tileRL vs sglang, same model, same H20 — 2026-08-28

> **Caveat (2026-08-28, later): throughput only.** The bf16 checkpoint sglang
> runs on emits garbage text (MMLU 0/1000, completions like `'束'`, `'tz'`) —
> see `docs/experience/errors/2026-08-28-sglang-bf16-checkpoint-garbage.md`.
> The numbers below compare kernels at the same shapes; they are NOT an
> accuracy comparison, and the tok/s should be re-confirmed once the
> conversion is fixed.

Same card (H20, driver 535.161.08, GPU 7), same weights (Qwen3.8-27B; sglang
cannot run its NVFP4 form on Hopper — `NotImplementedError: Current platform
does not support w4a4 nvfp4` — so it ran the dequantized bf16 checkpoint,
`scripts/dequant_to_bf16.py`, as bf16 and with online `--quantization fp8`).
sglang 0.5.13.post2 @ `0e7e68b76` (the commit the installed
`sglang-kernel 0.4.2.post2+cu129` pins), `python -m sglang.bench_one_batch
--batch-size 1 8 --input-len 512 --output-len 64`; tileRL numbers are the
harness's d512 rows (steady-state median of 3×20 ticks) and prefill/len512.
Host loadavg 7–9 for all runs.

| | bytes/tick | B=1 decode tok/s | B=8 decode agg tok/s | prefill tok/s (512) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **tileRL** NVFP4+FP8 (native) | 22.8 GB | **90.9** | 308.6 | 1836 |
| sglang bf16 | 42 GB | 54.2 | **387.0** | 2512 (B=1) / 1949 (B=8) |

## Reading

- **B=1 decode: tileRL 1.68× sglang-bf16 and 2.3× sglang-fp8.** Bandwidth
  explains the bf16 gap (42 vs 22.8 GB per tick, at similar ~60% efficiency:
  sglang bf16 reads 42 GB in 18.5 ms = 2.3 TB/s = 70%; we read 22.8 GB in
  11.0 ms = 2.1 TB/s = 64%). sglang's online-fp8 path is slower than its bf16
  at M=1 on this card — its fp8 decode GEMM is not a GEMV-class kernel.
- **B=8 decode: sglang bf16 wins, 387 vs 309.** Its bf16 tensor-core GEMMs
  at M=8 cost 20.7 ms — barely more than its M=1 tick — while our mma8
  decode GEMMs sit at 21–25% occupancy (128 regs/lane) and the tick is 27.9
  ms. The bytes advantage (2×) is not yet turned into B=8 time. Lever named
  in `wins/2026-08-28-mma8-decode-gemm.md`: register control.
- **Prefill: sglang fp8 is 2.2–2.7× ahead** (4–5k vs 1.8k tok/s); its bf16
  prefill ≈ ours. Prefill got no work today.
- Not compared: long-context decode (our 32k row is 78.6 tok/s; not run on
  sglang), speculative decoding (neither), serving overheads (both offline).

## What this licenses

For c=1 latency on one H20 with this model, tileRL is the fastest engine
measured here (Arle 84.5, sglang 54.2, tileRL 90.9). For throughput at B≥8
and for prefill, sglang is ahead; those are the next two projects, and the
comparison harness (`/work/sgl_bench2.sh`) reruns in ~5 minutes.
