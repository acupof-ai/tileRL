# fp8 GEMV: e4m3→bf16x2 bit placement + packed FMA, loads forced in flight — cuda(H20), 2026-08-28

> Status: Shipped

## Context

After the glue removal the fp8 GEMV (GDN in/out projections + lm_head) was
~48% of the B=1 tick's GPU time. ncu in the real model: 7.3 instr/elem
(scalar e4m3→f32 and bf16→f32 cvts + FMA), SM issue 76–85% busy, DRAM 54%.

## What Worked

- `tl_fp8_gemv_tiles<G>` (C, `T.call_extern`): e4m3 pair → bf16x2 is a bit
  placement, `(b&0x80)<<8 | (b&0x7f)<<4`, times 2^120 (`0x7B80`) to rebias
  the exponent — subnormals rebias exactly (same trick as the e2m1 twiddle);
  `prmt` spreads the 4 bytes of a word into two lane pairs; 8 `fma.rn.bf16x2`
  per 16-elem tile; one f32 scale-FMA per tile.
- The tile loads straight from the global pointers — tilelang locals handed
  to an extern by pointer land in local memory (first cut was slower than
  the scalar kernel).
- Loads are `asm volatile ld.global.nc.v4`: nvcc sinks plain `__ldg`s next
  to their use, so GROUP tiles never actually had GROUP loads in flight.

ncu, real model (base clocks): in_proj 16384×5120 **44.7 → 34.7 µs**,
out_proj 5120×6144 **19.1 → 15.2 µs**; instructions 19.2M → 11.7M; DRAM
54 → 61%. Occupancy is still register-limited (7 blocks/SM) — the next step
on this kernel.

- The fp4 tile kernel got the same structure (`tl_fp4_gemv_tiles<G>`, global
  loads, volatile): **neutral** on the tick (81.1 vs 82.0, noise) — it was
  already at 64% DRAM with occupancy 68%; kept for one load discipline.

- n_partition (rows per block) sweep on the tick, both GEMVs: **2 ≈ 4 > 8 > 16**
  (82.1 / 82.0 / 77.7 / 65.9 tok/s at d512). 4 stays.

## Rule

The eager `benchkit.ab` harness floors at ~40 µs of CPU launch time: every
arm of this kernel read "42 µs" — including a 16 MB shape that runs in 15.
Price GEMVs under ncu or in-graph only. And when an extern takes data, hand
it global pointers, not tilelang locals.
