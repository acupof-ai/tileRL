## What did not work: the task

Two of five manifest gates FAIL: `groups_untied` at **0.81** (threshold 0.5) and
`reward_rises` (0.800 → 0.775). `mmlu_holds`, `ce_falls` and `gsm8k_improves`
pass — the last of them vacuously, see below.

`tied_group_fraction` **0.81** — `groups_untied` FAILS at threshold 0.5. Of the
81 tied steps, **73 tie at the ceiling** (reward 1.0) and 8 at the floor. A tied
group has zero advantage and contributes no gradient, so 81% of the run's
compute bought nothing.

It climbed, and the shape is the finding:

| steps | tied | mean reward |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–10 | 0.50 | 0.675 |
| 11–20 | 0.50 | 0.825 |

This reproduces [P1 ties at the ceiling](../errors/2026-09-03-p1-ties-at-the-ceiling.md)

prompts solved at cap 2048.

## The one number the run establishes about the task

**GSM8K +5.6 points, 95% CI [+2.0, +9.2] — and the same accuracy at 2.74x fewer
tokens.** Four arms, all n=500, `--seed 0`, the same test file, greedy:

| eval cap | adapter | correct | accuracy | tokens |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 256 | no | 192/500 | 38.4% | 119360 (mean 238.7) |
| 256 | yes | 459/500 | 91.8% | 65801 (mean 131.6) |
| **2048** | **no** | **440/500** | **88.0%** | 173352 (394.0/correct) |
| **2048** | **yes** | **468/500** | **93.6%** | 67279 (143.8/correct) |

The comparison is the last two: one variable, the adapter. Everything above them
is the eval cap measuring itself.

**The capped pair's +53.4 points was 90% broken control.** Removing the cap is
worth **+49.6 points to the base arm and +1.8 to the trained one**, because the
base policy averages 238.7 tokens against a 256 ceiling and is cut mid-derivation
on a large share of problems. Its true accuracy is 88.0%, not 38.4%. This is
[the eval cap measured itself](../errors/2026-09-04-the-eval-cap-measured-itself.md)
reproducing at a second seed; #104 fixed the wiring, and the capped rows are kept
here only to show the size of the artefact.

**Tokens per correct answer 394.0 → 143.8, 2.74x**, is the larger effect and the
one this run actually bought. The policy did not get much more accurate. It got
much shorter at nearly the same accuracy — which is what a reward that is already
satisfied 81% of the time can still teach.

The interval is **unpaired**. Both arms scored the same 500 problems, so McNemar
would be tighter; the eval path logs only totals, so the pairing is not
recoverable. Per-problem rows land before the next comparison.

**This is also why the ties are not a defect.** A base policy at 88.0% solves
almost every group outright, so 81% of groups tie at the ceiling because the task
is finished, not because the run broke.

## Two numbers that are NOT results

- **MMLU 75.2% → 75.7%.** At n=1000 the 95% interval on a difference is ±3.8
  points. +0.5 is no movement.
- **reward 0.800 → 0.775 and tokens 170.5 → 139.9** (the manifest's own 25-step
  windows, `len(hist)//4`). `reward_rises` FAILS on the first pair. Neither is a
  training effect either way: both sit inside the per-step spread of a run where
  81% of steps carried no gradient, so the failure is as uninformative as a pass
  would have been.

**The CE column cannot be read alone.** It appears to climb 1.52 → 5.09 and that
is not degradation: corr(CE, completion tokens) = **−0.99**. CE is a per-token
mean and the short completions are the high-CE ones (step 9, 44 tokens, CE 5.09;
step 10, 247 tokens, CE 1.71).

**52% of the keys are dead**, not the ~30% two of us estimated. The param share
stays small because `.wscale` is 0.41 M across 466 keys — tiny tensors, but they
are what blocks a load.

This closes the gap left open by
[half the LoRA adapters trained nothing](../errors/2026-09-05-half-the-lora-adapters-trained-nothing.md).
The estimate there was 200.8 M (132.7 live / 68.1 dead) against 170.76 M actual;
the [thinking cap](2026-09-04-the-thinking-cap.md)'s recorded 170.8 M / 341 MB was
right all along.

**Two agreeing measurements of the wrong partition.** Two sessions bucketed this
file independently and got identical live/dead figures — both wrong, because the
second re-derived the first's *buckets* (`.scale`, `.conv1d`) rather than the
file's *suffixes*, and `.wscale` was in neither. Agreement between two derivations
sharing a premise tests the arithmetic, not the premise. What found it was
`--load-adapter` refusing: 1090 unknown keys, then 466 after a partial strip.

This run launched from `91977a8`, before the fix landed at `863a257`, so all 1090
dead adapters are in this checkpoint — carried through 100 steps at two AdamW
moments each.

## Rule

**GSM8K no longer trains the no-think 27B, and the reason is measured, not
inferred: the base policy already scores 88.0% uncapped.** Three independent runs
put the tied-group fraction at 0.81–0.86, all at the ceiling, which is what an
already-solved task looks like. 100 steps bought +5.6 points [+2.0, +9.2] and a
2.74x cut in tokens per correct answer. Move to a task the policy does not
already solve. A GRPO step at group 8 costs ~57 s on one H20;
budget from that, not from an estimate.

**And an eval that reports only a total cannot be compared paired.** Both arms
here scored the same 500 problems and the interval is still the unpaired one,
which is wider than the data supports, because nothing wrote down which problem
went which way.
